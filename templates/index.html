      Anatomy of a Fake Offer Letter

      Scammers forge corporate letterheads (TCS, Wipro, Infosys, Amazon) and demand "refundable medical examination" or "laptop insurance fees" before the joining date. Real companies NEVER charge candidates.

      Advance Fee Forged Seals Direct Deposit
      Task Scam

      Telegram & WhatsApp "Daily Task" Fraud

      Victims are promised ₹2,000–₹10,000 daily for liking videos, rating hotel listings, or writing fake reviews. Scammers pay small amounts at first, then demand "VIP prepaid investments" that cannot be withdrawn.

      Telegram Groups Ponzi Trap Crypto/UPI
      Domain Spoofing

      Domain Typosquatting & Lookalike Portals

      Attackers register domains like amazon-careers-verify.com or infosys-jobs-portal.xyz or use free Gmail accounts to deceive candidates into thinking they are speaking to HR.

      Lookalike URLs Free Webmails Credential Phishing
      SMS & Chat Trap

      Unsolicited SMS & WhatsApp Offers

      Receiving "Congratulations! Your resume was shortlisted for a 2-hour remote job" from unknown international country codes (+62, +234, +1, +44) without ever applying is an automatic red flag.

      Unsolicited SMS WhatsApp Recruiters Identity Theft
      Golden Rules

      Safe Job Search Golden Rules

      1. Only apply through official .com or verified corporate ATS portals.
      2. Interviews are conducted face-to-face or via enterprise video (Teams, Zoom, Meet), NEVER pure text on Telegram.
      3. If they ask you for even $1, it is 100% a scam.
